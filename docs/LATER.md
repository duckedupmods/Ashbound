# Later: things to add

This is a parking list, not a plan. Nothing here is built. Pick from it when a slice is done.

## Relics and curios (worn in extra slots)

The Curios API is already in the pack (Cataclysm needs it). It gives ring, necklace, belt, charm, back and head slots.

| Kind | Slot | What it does | Where it drops | Example |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ring | ring x2 | small stat: +5% damage of one element, +stamina, +guard | dungeon elites, tower chests | Ring of the Drowned (Undertow damage +6%) |
| Amulet | necklace | one passive with a trigger | bosses (rare) | Hangman's Knot: below 20% HP, 3 s of no knockback |
| Belt | belt | a carry or flask perk | towers, quests | Alchemist's Sash: +1 flask charge |
| Charm | charm | an on-kill or on-dodge effect | gate spill, bounties | Crow Feather: a perfect dodge leaves a decoy |
| Cloak | back | a look and one resistance | crafted at the Gate Forge | Rimeward Mantle: frost resist, frost trail |
| Relic | charm (one only) | an active skill on a key, long cooldown | S/SS bosses, tower tops | Heart of the Titan: slam the ground, 60 s |
| Sigil | head | changes how a weapon class plays | the skill tree's keystones | Sigil of the Reaper: scythes chain kills |

Rules:
- One relic at a time.
- Rings and amulets roll 1-2 random lines (like weapon affixes).
- Every dungeon gets one ring and one amulet in its theme.

## Other items

| Item | What | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Flasks | refillable heal and mana (Estus-like), refilled at a bonfire or shrine | replaces food healing in fights |
| Whetstones and oils | coat a weapon in an element for 2 minutes | crafted from dungeon common mats |
| Gate keys | a key from a boss opens a sealed vault in the overworld | ties gates to world structures |
| Trophies | a boss head to place as a block | the guild hall shows them |
| Maps | a torn map points to a tower or ruin | sold by Brann, found in chests |
| Runes | slot into weapons: +1 effect each | needs a rune bench |
| Pets and mounts | a tamed elite as a mount (wolf, drake) | far later |
| Spell scrolls | one-shot spells for non-mages | random chest loot |
| Titles | earned names shown over the head | "Towerbreaker", "Gate Warden" |
